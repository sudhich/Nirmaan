# Few-Shot Prompting

Few-shot prompting gives the model examples before asking it to solve a new problem.

```text
Classify each review as Positive, Negative, or Neutral.

Example 1:
Review: "The product is excellent."
Answer: Positive

Example 2:
Review: "The product is terrible."
Answer: Negative

Example 3:
Review: "The product arrived yesterday."
Answer: Neutral

Now classify:
Review: "The product works very well and I am happy with it."

Answer:
```

Expected:
`Positive`
