# Zero-Shot Examples

## Example 1

Input:
`The product quality is excellent.`

Prompt:
`Classify the sentiment as Positive, Negative, or Neutral.`

Possible output:
`Positive`

## Example 2

Input:
`My application crashes whenever I upload a CSV file.`

Prompt:
`Classify this support issue as Billing, Technical, Account, or Other.`

Possible output:
`Technical`
